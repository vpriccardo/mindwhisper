Mindwhisper

Choose how you send or recover a hidden message.

Same room

Transmit Ambient watermark for phones in the same room. Plays until you press Stop. Receive Listen with the microphone and recover the hidden text.

Voice / video call

Transmit Call-path ambient for WhatsApp, FaceTime, Teams, or telephone. Receive Hold near the remote call speaker — recover from the call audio.

No message leaves either phone except as sound. Microphone audio never uploads. Nothing is sent to a server. Works offline after the first load.
